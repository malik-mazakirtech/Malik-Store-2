MALIK STORE — VIDEO RECONSTRUCTION

1. Extract the WHOLE ZIP to a new folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Keep style.css, script.js and the assets folder beside index.html.

CSS is in style.css at the top level. JavaScript is in script.js.
No installation, npm command, backend or build is needed to open the website.

For GitHub, upload ALL contents of this folder, including assets.
Do not upload only the HTML files. Do not upload the ZIP itself as website source.

This package contains NO Dockerfile.

The contact form and shopping cart are demos: they do not send emails,
charge money or place real orders. Call/email links open external apps.
