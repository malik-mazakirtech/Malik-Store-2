Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains how Malik Store collects and uses your information.

1. Information We Collect

The contact form accepts your name, email address and message. In this demo, the entries stay in your browser and are not submitted to a server.

2. How We Use Information

Form entries are used only to display a local demonstration response. No real orders are processed.

3. Cookies Policy

This website does not set tracking cookies. Your chosen theme and demo cart may be saved in local browser storage.

4. Data Protection

No payment information is requested. Clearing browser storage removes saved theme and cart preferences.

5. Third-Party Services

Phone, email and directions links open external services. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

6. Changes to Policy

This policy should be updated if a real contact backend, analytics or checkout is added.
